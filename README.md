## 🚀 About ApexMath

ApexMath is more than just a calculator. It's a Swiss Army knife for mathematics, bringing essential and advanced mathematical tools together into one unified application.

From everyday arithmetic to algebra, geometry, matrices, complex numbers, calculus, and graphing, ApexMath is designed to make mathematical exploration accessible from a single workspace.

Whether you're solving equations, exploring functions, studying calculus, or recording mathematical discoveries, ApexMath brings the tools together without needing to jump between multiple applications.

Built with a focus on functionality, learning, and mathematical exploration, ApexMath aims to turn a calculator into an entire mathematical toolkit.

## ✨ Features

- 🧮 **Scientific Calculator** — Arithmetic, powers, roots, logarithms, trigonometry, factorials, fractions, and more.
- 📐 **Geometry** — Shape calculations, triangle solving, and coordinate geometry.
- 🧠 **Algebra** — Equation solving, polynomial tools, systems of equations, and factorization.
- 🔢 **Matrices** — Matrix operations, determinants, inverses, and more.
- 🌀 **Complex Numbers** — Explore and calculate with complex mathematics.
- 📈 **Graphing** — Visualize mathematical functions and explore their behavior.
- ∫ **Calculus** — Differentiation, integration, limits, and numerical methods.
- 📚 **Interactive Learning** — Lessons, worked examples, practice, and mathematical exploration.
- 📝 **Notebook** — Save equations, calculations, notes, and mathematical discoveries.
- 📱 **Offline-first** — Designed to work locally without depending on an internet connection.
